# AccessRelay demo script — target 1:45

## 0:00–0:15 — The problem

“A hotel can call a room accessible while leaving out the measurement that decides whether a traveler can use it. AccessRelay replaces that vague label with a room-specific evidence trail.”

Show the real Royal Lancaster comparison and the traveler’s door, bed, route, shower and transfer requirements.

## 0:15–0:35 — Real inventory and conservative research

“The property, location, image and room names come from LiteAPI sandbox inventory. The traveler adds the official page for the exact room. Firecrawl reads it through its Convex component, and OpenAI’s gpt-oss-120b proposes facts. Deterministic checks require the exact quote, room and unit. This page produced zero acceptable claims, so AccessRelay kept every detail unknown.”

Show the research event with zero claims. This failed-open moment is the product’s strongest trust demonstration.

## 0:35–1:00 — Human-approved outreach

“AccessRelay turns only the gaps into precise questions. The traveler reviews the recipient and every word before approving one message. AgentMail’s Convex component owns the durable send, bounded retries, thread state and signed webhook.”

Show the approval, sent and reply-received entries in Activity.

## 1:00–1:25 — Evidence comes back live

“The reply must match the approved hotel address and thread. The model proposes five statements, but the verifier admits only text the hotel actually wrote. Convex updates the comparison in real time, while availability stays separate from physical room evidence.”

Open one confirmed finding and show its exact quote, source, room and date.

## 1:25–1:45 — Why it matters

“AccessRelay does not promise suitability and does not sell a room. It gives travelers a clearer record before they spend money: what the hotel published, what it confirmed, what conflicts, and what is still unknown.”

End on the comparison and the line “Every selected requirement has evidence.”

## Disclosure for this recording

The production proof uses a second AgentMail inbox controlled by AccessRelay as the recipient. It proves delivery, reply threading, webhook verification and evidence processing. It is not a response from Royal Lancaster London.
